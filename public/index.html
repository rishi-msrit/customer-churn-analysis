About this page
  • Churn vs. RetainedShows what share of the 7,043 customers left the service. The percentage inside the ring is the overall churn rate across the full dataset.
  • Churn Rate by ContractMonth-to-month customers can leave at any time, producing much higher churn rates compared to customers on one- or two-year agreements.
  • Tenure DistributionOverlapping histograms of how long churners and retained customers had been on the service. Most churn concentrates within the first 12 months.
  • Monthly Charges DistributionBill amount distribution for both groups. Churners cluster at higher charge levels, often linked to fibre optic internet plans.
