About this page
  • How it worksThe sliders and checkboxes feed values into a logistic regression model trained on this dataset. The coefficients were exported and the probability is computed in your browser. No data leaves your device.
  • Most influential inputsTenure and contract type drive the prediction most strongly. Try switching from month-to-month to a two-year contract to see the probability drop.
  • What is driving thisThe five bars show which features pushed the probability up (red) or down (green) from the model baseline. Bar length reflects relative impact.
  • Total ChargesNot shown as a separate input. It is automatically derived from tenure times monthly charges, matching how the model was trained.
