About this page
  • PCA ScatterThree clustering dimensions (tenure, monthly charges, predicted churn probability) are reduced to two axes for plotting. Each dot is one customer; colour shows their segment. Hover for individual details.
  • Churn Rate by SegmentThe actual observed churn rate within each k-means cluster, ordered from lowest to highest risk. This confirms the clusters correspond to meaningful real-world retention categories.
  • Segment CardsEach card summarises one cluster's average profile and suggests a retention action. Segments are labelled based on whether average charges and churn probability fall above or below the dataset medians.
