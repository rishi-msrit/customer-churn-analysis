About this page
  • Statistical FindingsChi-square tests measure association between categorical variables and churn. Point-biserial r measures correlation for numeric variables. All findings here are significant at p less than 0.001.
  • Tenure BandChurn rate grouped into four time windows. The sharp drop between the 0-12 month band and later bands is the clearest pattern in the dataset.
  • Payment MethodElectronic check users churn at roughly double the rate of automatic-payment customers, likely reflecting lower commitment or billing friction.
  • Service Add-onsFor each service, the bars compare churn rate for customers who have it versus those who do not. Security and support add-ons show the strongest protective effect.
  • Contract x Internet HeatmapCombined view of two variables. Fibre optic on a month-to-month contract is the single highest-risk combination in the dataset.
